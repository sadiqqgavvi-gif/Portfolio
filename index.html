SmartHire

An AI interview coach. Practice technical, behavioral and situational questions by typing or speaking, and get every answer scored with a plan to improve.

The idea

Interview practice usually stops at reading questions. SmartHire makes you answer them, out loud if you want, then scores each answer from 1 to 10 with strengths, weaknesses and a concrete improvement plan, and tracks your progress over time.

What I built

  • Practice & mock modesTechnical, Behavioral and Situational categories with selectable difficulty and 5 to 20 questions, or a focused 10-question mock interview.
  • Voice answersThe Web Speech API transcribes answers live and can read questions and feedback back to you.
  • Three-tier AI scoringOpenAI first, an optional Python evaluator next, then a built-in Node.js evaluator, so feedback never goes down.
  • Guardrailed scoresAnswers that are too short, off-topic or gibberish are capped, whichever evaluator scored them.
  • Secure sessionsbcrypt passwords, Google sign-in, short-lived JWTs and rotating httpOnly refresh tokens with silent renewal.
  • Dashboard & DevOpsRecharts analytics for scores and history, Helmet, CORS, rate limits, health checks, Docker and CI on every push.

How an answer gets scored

  3. Tier 2Python evaluatorHeuristic fallback when the OpenAI tier is unavailable.

Chat App

One-on-one and group chat that updates instantly, with typing indicators, online presence and read receipts, and no page refresh.

The idea

A messaging app built to learn real-time systems properly. Every user and every chat gets its own Socket.io room, so messages, typing indicators and receipts reach exactly the right people the moment they happen.

What I built

  • Instant deliverySocket.io rooms per user and per chat deliver messages with no refresh.
  • Typing & presenceSee who is online and when someone is typing, in direct and group chats.
  • Read receiptsOne check for sent, two for delivered, colored checks once the message is read.
  • Message actionsReply, copy, edit, delete for me and delete for everyone.
  • AttachmentsImages, files and voice notes recorded right in the browser.
  • Groups & controlGroup admin tools, block and unblock, per-chat unread counts and a persisted dark mode.

MintList

Turns Chrome's new-tab page into a freeform bookmark workspace: drag boards anywhere, search everything with Ctrl/Cmd+K and theme it with your own wallpaper.

Ctrl+K search every bookmark

The idea

Bookmark bars get crowded and folders hide things. MintList puts links on boards you can drag anywhere on a canvas, grouped across named pages, and keeps it all locally in Chrome with no servers or accounts.

What I built

  • Freeform boardsDrag boards anywhere on the canvas and drag links between them. Reset layout snaps everything back into a grid.
  • PagesOrganize boards across named pages, rename them in place and switch from the sidebar.
  • Command paletteCtrl/Cmd+K searches every title and URL across all pages, with arrow keys and Enter to jump.
  • Wallpaper themingBuilt-in dark and light wallpapers or your own upload, and the whole UI re-themes to match.
  • Privacy blurURLs blur for screen sharing and show on hover. Try it: github.com/sadiqqgavvi-gif
  • Backup & restoreExport every page, board, link and setting to one JSON file and import it anywhere.
